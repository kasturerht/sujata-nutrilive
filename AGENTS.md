## ⚡ 1. Token Optimization & Brevity Rules
* **Be Ultra-Concise**: Responses must be short, technical, and to the point. No fluff or boilerplate text.
* **Targeted Reads & Diffs**: Never read whole files unless necessary; use line ranges. Always write targeted diffs.
* **Component Locations**:
  * Global Layout: `src/layouts/StoreLayout.astro`
  * Cart & Checkout logic: `src/components/CartDrawer.astro`
  * Header & Pincode: `src/components/StoreHeader.astro`
  * Educational / SEO Cluster: `src/pages/learn/`
  * Product Dynamic Pages: `src/pages/product/[handle].astro`
